Trial and delivery

The paid plans include an advertised seven-day trial and require a payment method. Check the trial end date, first charge and monthly or annual renewal at Gumroad checkout. Licence in short

The Runtime licence identifies Johannes Meyer as the licensor.

  • A standard licence covers one person, one device and one local user account.
  • Activation and renewal use the internet. Access follows the subscription status.
  • For a device move, use onesimulation-license release-device on the old device and activate on the new device.

Read the English translation of the Runtime 0.2.1a8 licence and third-party notices included in the wheel.
