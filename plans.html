Trial and delivery

Gumroad offers a seven-day trial on paid plans and asks for a payment method. Manage or cancel the subscription through your Gumroad receipt. Choose the ZIP for macOS on Apple Silicon, Ubuntu 22.04 x86-64, or Windows x64. Each ZIP contains the compiled wheel and README.md. Gumroad provides your personal licence key in the receipt or on the download page. See the Quickstart for installation and activation.

The download includes the optional MCP server. Connect OneSimulation to Codex to start saved studies, follow their progress and retrieve results from your own machine.

Licence in short

  • A standard licence covers one person, one device and one local user account.
  • Activation and renewal use the internet. Access follows the subscription status.
  • For a device move, use onesimulation-license release-device on the old device and activate on the new device.
  • The wheel contains the licence terms and third-party notices.
