Licence in short

  • The licence is personal and covers one person, one device and one local user account.
  • It lasts while the subscription is active. The activation renews itself online; there is no perpetual licence.
  • To move to another computer, free the seat with onesimulation-license release-device and activate on the new one.
  • The runtime is delivered as a binary package. Ready-to-run example notebooks are free in the Showcase and yours to change.
