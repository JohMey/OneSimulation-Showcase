Multibody dynamics · Reduced rigid-body ODE · angular-velocity trajectory

A numerically integrated Euler-top trajectory in angular-velocity space

Simulate rigid-body motion, pendulums, joint systems, four-bar mechanisms and torsional drivetrains.

Rigid-body dynamicsPendulumsJoint systemsMechanismsKinematicsDrivetrains

How it was computed

  • 20,000 RK4 steps; three angular-velocity state components; 2001 saved states
  • Actual EulerTop integration · axes are angular-velocity components · reduced ODE, not a 3D volume mesh
