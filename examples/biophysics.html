Localized heating in perfused tissue

Explore perfused-tissue heating, passive membranes, lumped compliant vessels, axial tissue elements and compartment clearance.

How it was computed

  • 8,192 1D finite-difference samples; 1,500 RK4 steps; 0–300 s; four independently calculated profiles
  • Native 1D Pennes simulation · zoom around two heat sources · synthetic tissue properties · no patient-specific calibration
