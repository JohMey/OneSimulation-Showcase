Hydrodynamics · Axisymmetric VSI simulation

A rotating disk. A million unknowns in motion.

Model incompressible and compressible flow, shallow water, porous flow, phase transport and viscoelastic response.

Incompressible flowCompressible flowShallow waterDarcy flowPhase transportReactive transportViscoelasticity

How it was computed

  • 512 × 512 finite-volume cells · 1,049,600 native unknowns · 15 orbits
  • QUICK spatial discretisation · BDF2 time integration · freshly initialized fine grid · axisymmetric, locally isothermal disk
Watch the calculated time evolution

32 saved states at 1920 × 1080 pixels. Fixed colour scales and no interpolated time frames.

Resolution and numerical checks

The final-orbit comparison with half the maximum time step changes the final native meridional velocity by 0.0426% in relative L2 norm and the radial velocity by 0.1544%. Relative mass and angular-momentum drift remain below 5 × 10−15.

The time comparison covers orbit 14.006 to 15. Axisymmetric, locally isothermal, inviscid finite disk segment in dimensionless units; this is not a full mesh-convergence study or a claim of resolved nonaxisymmetric 3D turbulence.
