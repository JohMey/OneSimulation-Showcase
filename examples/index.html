Example notebooks

Each notebook builds a standard physics case with OneSimulation, solves it and grades the result against a closed-form or independent reference. Filter by field, open a case for its description, and download it as a Jupyter notebook or a plain Python script, free and without sign-up.

The dimension tag names the computational model: 3D means a full three-dimensional mesh and solver, 0D a lumped balance. Many cases are chosen because a closed-form solution exists, so a 3D solve often verifies an effectively one-dimensional or axisymmetric field - that is the point of a reference case, not a limitation of the solver.
