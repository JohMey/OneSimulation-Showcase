Bolted busbar assembly geometry

A copper busbar with two bends, two bolt holes, two washers and two bolts, built as an assembly with named parts and contact faces.

Swept sectionAssemblyContact facesClosed-form check

Run locally with OneSimulation. The example downloads are free. Running them requires an activated runtime. Runtime Alpha 0.2.1a7 · CPython 3.13 on macOS (Apple Silicon), Ubuntu 22.04 (x86-64), and Windows x64. Installation and activation · View pilot plans. Scope of the reference checks.

Download the notebook (.ipynb)Download the script (.py)

What it computes

OneSimulation sweeps the bar section along a path with rounded corners, drills the holes, places washers and bolts and joins all five parts in an assembly that lists the faces where two parts touch; every part is meshed with tetrahedra.

What it checks

Path length 287.1239 mm and the volumes of bar, washers and bolts match their closed forms to 1e-15, and the four contact faces have exactly the closed-form ring areas of 137.4447 mm2 (washer on bar) and 77.3146 mm2 (bolt head on washer).

The notebook

The complete notebook, cell by cell. Run it to produce the figures and the comparison printout.

Busbars carry large currents between the components of switchgear and power electronics; their temperature is set by Joule heating in the bar and by the contact resistance at the bolted joints. A model of a busbar therefore needs the bar, the bolts and washers as separate parts and the faces where they touch.

partbuilt from
busbarSweep of a 40 x 6 mm rectangle along a Z-shaped path with corners of 15 mm radius, less two holes of 9 mm
washer_1, washer_2AnnularCylinder, 16 / 8.4 mm diameter, 1.6 mm thick, on the bar
bolt_1, bolt_2UnionFromObjects of a head (13 mm, 5.5 mm high) and a shank (8 mm, 30 mm long) through the hole

Assembly(parts=[...]) keeps one volume per part and lists the faces where two parts touch, assembly.interfaces, the faces for contact or thermal contact conditions.

Reference. The path of segments \(l_1, l_2, l_3\) with two corners of radius \(R\) has the length \(L = l_1 + l_2 + l_3 - 4R + \pi R\); the bar has \(V = b t L - 2\pi r_h^2 t\). A washer touches the bar on the ring between the hole and its outer edge, \(\pi(r_w^2 - r_h^2)\), and the bolt head on the ring \(\pi(r_k^2 - r_i^2)\) between the inner edge of the washer and the edge of the head.

import numpy as np
from onesim.cad.d2 import Profile
from onesim.cad.d3 import AnnularCylinder, Assembly, Cylinder, Sweep, UnionFromObjects
from onesim.meshing import TetrahedralMesher
from onesim.meshing.quality import quality
from onesim.visualization import plots, theme

theme.set()

Parts

The bar runs 120 mm along x, rises 80 mm and runs 100 mm on; the section is centred on the path. The holes sit 30 mm from each end.

breadth, thickness, corner, hole = 0.04, 0.006, 0.015, 0.0045
segments = (0.12, 0.08, 0.10)
path = [(0.0, 0.0, 0.0), (0.12, 0.0, 0.0), (0.12, 0.0, 0.08), (0.22, 0.0, 0.08)]
bar = Sweep(
    profile=Profile.rectangle(
        breadth, thickness, origin=(-breadth / 2, -thickness / 2)
    ),
    path=path,
    corner_radius=corner,
)
holes = [(0.03, 0.0, 0.0), (0.19, 0.0, 0.08)]
for index, (x, y, z) in enumerate(holes, start=1):
    bar = bar - Cylinder(radius=hole, length=0.02, origin=(x, y, z - 0.01)).named(
        f"hole_{index}"
    )
washer_outer, washer_inner, washer_thickness = 0.008, 0.0042, 0.0016
head_radius, head_height, shank_radius, shank_length = 0.0065, 0.0055, 0.004, 0.03
parts = [bar.named("busbar")]
for index, (x, y, z) in enumerate(holes, start=1):
    top = z + thickness / 2
    parts.append(
        AnnularCylinder(
            inner_radius=washer_inner,
            outer_radius=washer_outer,
            length=washer_thickness,
            origin=(x, y, top),
        ).named(f"washer_{index}")
    )
    parts.append(
        UnionFromObjects(
            objects=[
                Cylinder(
                    radius=head_radius,
                    length=head_height,
                    origin=(x, y, top + washer_thickness),
                ),
                Cylinder(
                    radius=shank_radius,
                    length=shank_length,
                    origin=(x, y, top + washer_thickness - shank_length),
                ),
            ]
        ).named(f"bolt_{index}")
    )
busbar = Assembly(parts=parts)
print(f"parts: {', '.join(part.id for part in busbar.volumes)}")

meshes = {
    part.id: TetrahedralMesher(target_size=0.004, curvature_angle=0.5).generate(
        busbar.part(part.id)
    )
    for part in busbar.volumes
}
view = plots.Camera(elevation=25, azimuth=-60)
fig = plots.figure(1, 2, size=(16, 7))
plots.geometry(
    busbar,
    selection={part.id: busbar.volume_of(part.id) for part in busbar.volumes},
    labels=False,
    camera=view,
    title="Busbar with washers and bolts",
    ax=fig[0],
)
plots.mesh(
    meshes["busbar"],
    groups=("all",),
    legend=False,
    camera=view,
    title=f"Mesh of the bar: {meshes['busbar'].cell_count} tetrahedra",
    ax=fig[1],
)
plots.show()

Results

Path length and volumes of every part, then every contact face of the assembly with its area.

length = sum(segments) - 4 * corner + np.pi * corner
closed = {
    "busbar": breadth * thickness * length - 2 * np.pi * hole**2 * thickness,
    **{
        f"washer_{k}": np.pi * (washer_outer**2 - washer_inner**2) * washer_thickness
        for k in (1, 2)
    },
    **{
        f"bolt_{k}": np.pi
        * (head_radius**2 * head_height + shank_radius**2 * shank_length)
        for k in (1, 2)
    },
}
print(
    f"path length {Sweep(profile=Profile.rectangle(breadth, thickness), path=path, corner_radius=corner).length * 1e3:.4f} mm, "
    f"closed form {length * 1e3:.4f} mm"
)
print("part        CAD [cm3]    mesh [cm3]   closed form [cm3]   deviation CAD")
for part in busbar.volumes:
    meshed = quality(meshes[part.id])["size"].sum()
    print(
        f"{part.id:9s} {part.measure * 1e6:11.5f} {meshed * 1e6:12.5f} {closed[part.id] * 1e6:16.5f} {part.measure / closed[part.id] - 1:+15.1e}"
    )
rings = {
    "busbar": np.pi * (washer_outer**2 - hole**2),
    "bolt": np.pi * (head_radius**2 - washer_inner**2),
}
print("contact                 area [mm2]   closed form [mm2]")
for contact in busbar.interfaces:
    other = "busbar" if "busbar" in contact.parts else "bolt"
    print(
        f"{' / '.join(contact.parts):22s} {contact.area * 1e6:11.4f} {rings[other] * 1e6:16.4f}"
    )

Figure

The copper bar with its two bends, and the mesh of the upper bolt cut through its axis.

fig = plots.figure(1, 2, size=(16, 7))
plots.render(
    bar, camera=view, material="copper", title="Copper busbar with two bends", ax=fig[0]
)
plots.mesh(
    meshes["bolt_2"],
    cell_values=quality(meshes["bolt_2"])["shape"],
    limits=(0, 1),
    plane=((0.19, 0.0, 0.08), (0.0, 1.0, 0.0)),
    label="shape measure",
    camera=plots.Camera(elevation=5, azimuth=90),
    title=f"Upper bolt cut through its axis: {meshes['bolt_2'].cell_count} tetrahedra",
    ax=fig[1],
)
plots.show()
