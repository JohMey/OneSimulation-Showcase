Heat transfer · 3D

Heat conduction with volumetric heating

A wall with uniform internal heating held at fixed surfaces; the parabolic temperature profile is graded against theory.

SteadyVolumetric sourceClosed-form check
Download the notebook (.ipynb)Download the script (.py)

What it computes

OneSimulation solves the steady heat equation with a uniform volumetric source on a 3D mesh; the two faces are fixed and the sides are insulated.

What it checks

The profile is compared with T(x) = T_s + q/(2k)·x(L-x); reproduced to finite-element precision.

What you need to run it. An installed, activated OneSimulation runtime (Python 3.13). As a student or course instructor, you have free access. Everyone else can start with the 7-day free trial. The notebook itself carries no key. These are numerical verifications against references, not experimental validation.
