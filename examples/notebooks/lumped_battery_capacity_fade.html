Capacity and power fade of a lumped battery

A 5 Ah lithium-ion cell aged over ten years at 25, 35 and 45 °C by a diffusion-limited SEI film and daily cycling.

Lumped batterySEI growthCycle fadeEvent location

Run locally with OneSimulation. The example downloads are free. Running them requires an activated runtime. Runtime Alpha 0.2.1a7 · CPython 3.13 on macOS (Apple Silicon), Ubuntu 22.04 (x86-64), and Windows x64. Installation and activation · View pilot plans. Scope of the reference checks.

Download the notebook (.ipynb)Download the script (.py)

What it computes

OneSimulation integrates the film growth with its SEI transport laws and adaptive BDF2, locates the end of life at 80 % capacity with an event, combines the calendar loss with an empirical cycle fade, and discharges the aged cells as lumped electrothermal batteries with the grown capacity loss and film resistance.

What it checks

The film follows the closed form sqrt(L0² + 2 V c D t) to 3e-4 nm (371, 482 and 615 nm after ten years); the end of life comes after 6.787, 5.417 and 4.200 years, equal to the root of the closed forms to four digits; the 5 A discharge energy falls from 17.60 to 12.88 Wh after nine years and matches Q∫U dS - I²R t to all printed digits.

The notebook

The complete notebook, cell by cell. Run it to produce the figures and the comparison printout.

Most of the calendar ageing of a lithium-ion cell comes from a parasitic reaction at the negative electrode: solvent molecules diffuse through the solid-electrolyte interphase (SEI) and react at the graphite surface. The film thickens, every mole of film product consumes two moles of cyclable lithium, and the film resistance grows:

\[\frac{dL}{dt}=\bar V\,\frac{c_\mathrm{sol}D(T)}{L},\qquad Q_\mathrm{loss}=\frac{2F\,A_s}{\bar V}\,(L-L_0),\qquad R=R_0+\frac{L}{\kappa_\mathrm{SEI}A_s},\qquad D(T)=D_\mathrm{ref}\,e^{-\frac{E_a}{R_g}\left(\frac1T-\frac1{T_\mathrm{ref}}\right)},\]

with the partial molar volume \(\bar V\) of the film, the solvent concentration \(c_\mathrm{sol}\) and the active surface \(A_s\) of the negative electrode. Cycling adds an empirical fade of the capacity, \(Q_\mathrm{cyc}(n)=Q_0\,e^{-k\,\max(n-n_0,0)}\) after \(n\) equivalent full cycles; the cell does one cycle per day, and \(Q=Q_\mathrm{cyc}(n)-Q_\mathrm{loss}\). The aged cell is a lumped electrothermal battery (the model of the constant-current example) with capacity \(Q\) and resistance \(R\).

Reference. The film law has the closed form \(L(t)=\sqrt{L_0^2+2\bar Vc_\mathrm{sol}D\,t}\), so the calendar loss grows with \(\sqrt t\) (M. B. Pinson and M. Z. Bazant, J. Electrochem. Soc. 160 (2013) A243; H. J. Ploehn, P. Ramadass and R. E. White, J. Electrochem. Soc. 151 (2004) A456). The end of life, 80 % of the initial capacity, follows from the closed forms by root finding. For a constant current \(I\) the lumped cell delivers \(E=Q\int_{s_1}^{s_0}U_\mathrm{oc}\,ds-I^2R\,t\) between the states of charge \(s_0\) and \(s_1\). The open-circuit curve and the fresh cell (5 Ah, 30 mΩ) follow the LG M50 cell of Chen et al., J. Electrochem. Soc. 167 (2020) 080534.

import numpy as np
from onesim.cad.geometry import LumpedDomain
from onesim.meshing import LumpedMesh
from onesim.numerics import Numerics
from onesim.numerics.discretization import LumpedParameters
from onesim.numerics.time import AdaptiveBDF, ClassicalRungeKutta4, EventDefinition
from onesim.physics.chemistry import (
    BatteryChemistry,
    BatteryCycleCapacityFade,
    LumpedBattery,
    sei_area_resistance,
    sei_thickness_rate,
    solvent_limited_sei_flux,
)
from onesim.results.storage import MemoryOutputSink, Output
from onesim.simulation import Problem, Simulation
from onesim.simulation.initial_conditions import InitialValue
from onesim.visualization import plots, theme
from scipy.optimize import brentq

theme.set()

Cell and ageing parameters

faraday, gas, year = 96485.33212, 8.314462618, 365.25 * 86400
q0, r0, surface = 5.0 * 3600, 0.030, 3.36
solvent, molar_volume, film0, kappa_film = 4541.0, 9.585e-5, 5e-9, 5e-6
d_ref, activation = 5e-22, 40e3
cycling = BatteryCycleCapacityFade(
    initial_capacity=q0, fade_rate=4e-5, induction_cycles=200
)
diffusivity = lambda T: d_ref * np.exp(-activation / gas * (1 / T - 1 / 298.15))
open_circuit = lambda s: (
    4.4875
    - 0.8090 * (y := 0.2661 + (1 - s) * 0.5829)
    - 0.0428 * np.tanh(18.5138 * (y - 0.5542))
    - 17.7326 * np.tanh(15.7890 * (y - 0.3117))
    + 17.5842 * np.tanh(15.9308 * (y - 0.3120))
    - 1.9793 * np.exp(-39.3631 * (x := 0.0279 + s * 0.8735))
    - 0.2482
    + 0.0909 * np.tanh(29.8538 * (x - 0.1234))
    + 0.04478 * np.tanh(14.9159 * (x - 0.2769))
    + 0.0205 * np.tanh(30.4444 * (x - 0.6103))
)

Ten years at 25, 35 and 45 °C

The film thickness (in nm, over time in years) is integrated with OneSimulation's adaptive BDF2 method, using the library's film laws; an event marks the end of life at 80 % of the initial capacity.

capacity = lambda years, film: cycling.remaining_capacity(
    365.25 * years
) - 2 * faraday * surface / molar_volume * (1e-9 * film - film0)
ageing = {}
for celsius in (25, 35, 45):
    D = diffusivity(273.15 + celsius)
    growth = (
        lambda t, y: year
        * 1e9
        * sei_thickness_rate(
            solvent_limited_sei_flux(solvent, D, 1e-9 * y), molar_volume
        )
    )
    end_of_life = EventDefinition(
        "end of life", lambda t, y: capacity(t, y[0]) - 0.8 * q0, direction=-1
    )
    run = AdaptiveBDF(
        order=2, relative_tolerance=1e-6, absolute_tolerance=1e-6, maximum_step=0.05
    ).integrate(
        growth,
        np.array([1e9 * film0]),
        0.0,
        10.0,
        initial_step=1e-6,
        events=(end_of_life,),
    )
    times, film = np.array(run.times), np.array(run.states)[:, 0]
    exact = lambda t, D=D: 1e9 * np.sqrt(
        film0**2 + 2 * molar_volume * solvent * D * t * year
    )
    ageing[celsius] = dict(
        times=times,
        film=film,
        exact=exact(times),
        capacity=capacity(times, film),
        life=[event.time for event in run.events],
        exact_life=brentq(lambda t: capacity(t, exact(t)) - 0.8 * q0, 1e-6, 50.0),
    )

Discharge of aged cells

The cell aged at 25 °C, taken after 0, 3, 6 and 9 years, discharged at 5 A from full to 2 % state of charge (the fresh cell's 1C).

cell = LumpedDomain()
numerics = Numerics(spatial=LumpedParameters(), time_integrator=ClassicalRungeKutta4())
discharges = {}
for age in (0, 3, 6, 9):
    film = np.interp(age, ageing[25]["times"], ageing[25]["film"])
    q, resistance = (
        capacity(age, film),
        r0 + float(sei_area_resistance(1e-9 * film, kappa_film)) / surface,
    )
    model = LumpedBattery(
        domain=cell.system,
        chemistry=BatteryChemistry(
            "aged",
            open_circuit_voltage=lambda s, T: open_circuit(s),
            internal_resistance=lambda s, T: resistance,
        ),
        charge_capacity=q,
        current=5.0,
        thermal_capacity=70.0,
        heat_transfer=0.3,
        ambient_temperature=298.15,
    )
    record = MemoryOutputSink()
    end = 0.98 * q / 5.0
    Simulation(
        problem=Problem(
            name="aged discharge",
            geometry=cell,
            physics=(model,),
            initial_conditions=(
                InitialValue(model.state_of_charge, 1.0),
                InitialValue(model.temperature, 298.15),
            ),
        ),
        mesh=LumpedMesh(cell),
        numerics=numerics,
    ).run(
        start_time=0.0,
        end_time=end,
        time_step=end / 1000,
        output=Output(
            fields=(model.terminal_voltage, model.temperature),
            interval=end / 1000,
            sink=record,
        ),
    )
    s = np.linspace(0.02, 1.0, 4001)
    discharges[age] = dict(
        times=record.times,
        voltage=record.time_series(model.terminal_voltage)[1],
        q=q,
        resistance=resistance,
        energy=5.0
        * np.trapezoid(record.time_series(model.terminal_voltage)[1], record.times),
        exact=q * np.trapezoid(open_circuit(s), s) - 25.0 * resistance * end,
        heating=record.time_series(model.temperature)[1].max() - 298.15,
    )

Results

for celsius, a in ageing.items():
    print(
        f"{celsius} °C: film after 10 years {a['film'][-1]:.2f} nm (closed form {a['exact'][-1]:.2f} nm, largest deviation "
        f"{np.abs(a['film'] - a['exact']).max():.1e} nm); capacity {a['capacity'][-1] / q0:.2%}; end of life after "
        + (
            f"{a['life'][0]:.4f} years (closed form {a['exact_life']:.4f})"
            if a["life"]
            else f"more than 10 years (closed form {a['exact_life']:.2f})"
        )
    )
print(
    "age [y]   capacity [Ah]   resistance [mΩ]   energy [Wh]   closed form [Wh]   deviation   heating [K]"
)
for age, d in discharges.items():
    print(
        f"{age:7d}   {d['q'] / 3600:13.3f}   {1e3 * d['resistance']:15.2f}   {d['energy'] / 3600:11.4f}   {d['exact'] / 3600:16.4f}"
        f"   {100 * (d['energy'] / d['exact'] - 1):+8.3f} %   {d['heating']:11.2f}"
    )
