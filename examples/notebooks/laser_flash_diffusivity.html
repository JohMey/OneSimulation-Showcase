Heat transfer · 3D

Laser-flash measurement of thermal diffusivity

A short energy pulse on the front face of an insulated steel disc; the rear-face rise is graded against Parker's series and the diffusivity recovered from the half-rise time.

TransientPulsed heat fluxParker series check
Download the notebook (.ipynb)Download the script (.py)

What it computes

OneSimulation solves the transient heat equation on a 3D hex mesh in two phases, a finely stepped flash with a time-dependent heat flux and a BDF2 diffusion phase restarted from the flash field, with every other face insulated.

What it checks

Deposited energy matches the pulse to 0.5 %, the rear-face curve deviates by at most 0.3 % of the full rise from Parker's series, and the recovered diffusivity is within 1 % of the material value.

What you need to run it. An installed, activated OneSimulation runtime (Python 3.13). As a student or course instructor, you have free access. Everyone else can start with the 7-day free trial. The notebook itself carries no key. These are numerical verifications against references, not experimental validation.
