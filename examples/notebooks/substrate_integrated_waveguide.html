A waveguide built into a circuit board

Two rows of plated vias guide a microwave signal through the board. Measure the signal on either side of cutoff and inspect its phase inside the guide.

STEP importVia fenceNédélec elementsTEM ports

Run locally with OneSimulation. The example downloads are free. Running them requires an activated runtime. OneSimulation 1.0 · CPython 3.13 on macOS (Apple Silicon), Ubuntu 22.04 (x86-64), and Windows x64. Installation and activation · View pilot plans. Scope of the reference checks.

Download the notebook (.ipynb)Download the script (.py)Download the geometry (.step)Download the geometry script (.py)

What it computes

A three-dimensional Maxwell model resolves the substrate, the microstrip tapers and the vias with conducting walls. Two ports measure reflection and transmission. A midplane slice shows the electric field at 12 GHz. The script that builds the STEP files comes with the notebook.

What it checks

Refinement from 38,395 to 73,139 tetrahedra (8,062 to 15,318 vertices) brings the calculated phase constant at 12 GHz from 251.0 to 249.7 rad/m, within 1.2 % of the equivalent-width prediction of 246.6 rad/m. With a predicted cutoff of 9.003 GHz, transmission rises from −21.98 dB at 8 GHz to −0.23 dB at 12 GHz.

The geometry script

The script substrate_integrated_waveguide_geometry.py builds the geometry with the CAD objects of OneSimulation and writes the files. Change a dimension there, run it, and run the notebook again.

"""Geometry of the OneSimulation example "Substrate integrated waveguide".

Builds with the CAD objects of OneSimulation a substrate integrated waveguide (SIW) on a 0.787 mm
substrate of relative permittivity 2.2: two rows of 12 plated vias (diameter 1.0 mm, pitch 1.8 mm,
rows 11.81 mm apart) under a top plate 23.4 mm long and 13.81 mm wide, fed at both ends by 50-ohm
microstrip lines
(2.42 mm wide, 5 mm long) through linear tapers 8 mm long that widen to 4.7 mm. The top plate
reaches 0.5 mm beyond the outer edges of the vias and covers them. The box of air above the board is
5 mm high. Four STEP files: substrate_integrated_waveguide.step (the air with the vias removed; faces
port_1 and port_2 at the ends of the feed lines, ground below, vias, open for the lid and the long
sides), substrate_integrated_waveguide_substrate.step (the substrate with the via holes),
substrate_integrated_waveguide_copper.step (a thin layer of air in the shape of the top copper, whose
face on the substrate is the copper) and substrate_integrated_waveguide_vias.step (the via barrels,
for the figures).
"""

import numpy as np
from onesim.cad.d2 import Profile
from onesim.cad.d3 import Box, Cylinder, Prism

height, permittivity, lid, layer = 0.787e-3, 2.2, 5e-3, 0.4e-3
diameter, pitch, vias, rows = 1.0e-3, 1.8e-3, 12, 11.81e-3
line, feed, taper, mouth, margin = 2.42e-3, 5e-3, 8e-3, 4.7e-3, 0.5e-3
plate = (vias - 1) * pitch + 2 * pitch
x0 = -(feed + taper + plate / 2)
side = rows / 2 + 6e-3
box = Box(length=-2 * x0, width=2 * side, height=lid, origin=(x0, -side, 0.0))
barrels = [Cylinder(radius=diameter / 2, length=height, origin=(-(vias - 1) * pitch / 2 + k * pitch, sign * rows / 2, 0.0))
           for k in range(vias) for sign in (1, -1)]
holes = barrels[0]
for barrel in barrels[1:]:
    holes = holes | barrel
air = box - holes
near = lambda value, target: abs(value - target) < 1e-9
faces = air.boundaries
air = air.grouped(
    port_1=faces.x_min, port_2=faces.x_max, ground=faces.z_min,
    vias=air.select(faces=lambda face: (face.kind == "cylinder" and abs(face.radius - diameter / 2) < 1e-6)
                    or (face.kind == "plane" and near(face.center[2], height))),
    open=faces.z_max | faces.y_min | faces.y_max,
)
air.save("substrate_integrated_waveguide.step")
(Box(length=-2 * x0, width=2 * side, height=height, origin=(x0, -side, 0.0)) - holes).save("substrate_integrated_waveguide_substrate.step")
strip = lambda x, y, dx, dy: Box(length=dx, width=dy, height=layer, origin=(x, y, height))
cone = lambda start, sign: Prism(profile=Profile.polygon([(start, -line / 2), (start + sign * taper, -mouth / 2), (start + sign * taper, mouth / 2), (start, line / 2)]),
                                 height=layer, origin=(0.0, 0.0, height))
span = rows + diameter + 2 * margin
copper = (strip(x0, -line / 2, feed + 1e-4, line) | cone(x0 + feed, 1) | strip(-plate / 2, -span / 2, plate, span)
          | cone(-x0 - feed, -1) | strip(-x0 - feed - 1e-4, -line / 2, feed + 1e-4, line))
copper.save("substrate_integrated_waveguide_copper.step")
holes.save("substrate_integrated_waveguide_vias.step")
effective_width = rows - diameter**2 / (0.95 * pitch)
print(f"effective width {effective_width * 1e3:.3f} mm, TE10 cutoff {299792458.0 / (2 * effective_width * np.sqrt(permittivity)) / 1e9:.3f} GHz")

The notebook

The complete notebook, cell by cell. Run it to produce the figures and the comparison printout.

A substrate integrated waveguide (SIW) is a rectangular waveguide built into a printed circuit board: the top and bottom copper are its broad walls, two rows of plated vias its narrow walls. Like a hollow waveguide it is a high-pass filter: below the cutoff of its TE\(_{10}\) mode nothing passes. Here the vias have a diameter of 1.0 mm and a pitch of 1.8 mm, the rows are 11.81 mm apart on a 0.787 mm substrate of relative permittivity 2.2; 50 Ω microstrip lines feed the guide through tapers that turn the microstrip field into the TE\(_{10}\) field.

OneSimulation solves the time-harmonic Maxwell equation

\[\nabla\times\big(\mu_r^{-1}\nabla\times\mathbf E\big)-k_0^2\,\varepsilon_r\,\mathbf E=0\]

with first-order Nédélec elements. The top copper is a sheet without thickness (region_interface of a thin layer of air shaped like it, lying over the via holes); the vias are holes in the substrate with perfectly conducting walls. The feed lines end in TEMPorts that compute their quasi-TEM mode from the electrostatic potential of the cross-section; port 1 is fed and scattering_parameters gives reflection and transmission in the impedance of the feed lines.

facescondition
port_1, port_2TEM port, signal conductor: the copper
ground, vias, copperperfect electric conductor
open (lid and long sides)impedance boundary \(Y_0=\sqrt{\varepsilon_0/\mu_0}\)

Reference (Y. Cassivi, L. Perregrini, P. Arcioni, M. Bressan, K. Wu, G. Conciauro, Dispersion characteristics of substrate integrated rectangular waveguide, IEEE MWCL 12 (2002) 333-335; D. Deslandes, K. Wu, IEEE MWCL 11 (2001) 68-70). For \(p/d<2\) and \(d/w<0.2\) the SIW behaves like a rectangular waveguide of width

\[w_{\rm eff}=w-\frac{d^2}{0.95\,p},\qquad f_c=\frac{c}{2\,w_{\rm eff}\sqrt{\varepsilon_r}}, \qquad \beta=\sqrt{\varepsilon_r k_0^2-(\pi/w_{\rm eff})^2},\]

11.225 mm and 9.003 GHz here. Above the cutoff the TE\(_{10}\) wave travels with the propagation constant \(\beta\) of that waveguide; below it the wave decays and the transmission collapses.

import numpy as np
from onesim.cad.d2 import Plane
from onesim.cad.exchange import load
from onesim.materials import VACUUM_PERMEABILITY, VACUUM_PERMITTIVITY
from onesim.materials.electromagnetic import IsotropicElectromagneticMaterial, RegionwiseElectromagneticMaterial
from onesim.meshing import LocalRefinement, TetrahedralMesher, region_interface
from onesim.numerics import Numerics
from onesim.numerics.fem import FiniteElement, Nedelec
from onesim.numerics.linear import ComplexSparseDirect
from onesim.physics.electromagnetics import (FrequencyDomainMaxwell, ImpedanceBoundary, PerfectElectricConductor, TEMPort,
                                             scattering_parameters)
from onesim.simulation import Problem, Simulation
from onesim.visualization import plots, theme

theme.set()

light, eta0 = 1 / np.sqrt(VACUUM_PERMEABILITY * VACUUM_PERMITTIVITY), np.sqrt(VACUUM_PERMEABILITY / VACUUM_PERMITTIVITY)
rows, diameter, pitch, permittivity, height = 11.81e-3, 1.0e-3, 1.8e-3, 2.2, 0.787e-3

Geometry and mesh

load reads the air with the vias removed, the substrate and the thin layer shaped like the top copper; substrate and layer become regions of one mesh. Mesh targets are 0.7 mm at the copper, 0.8 mm in the substrate, 0.22 mm at the vias and 1.3 mm in air. This increases the mesh from 38,395 to 73,139 tetrahedra and from 8,062 to 15,318 vertices.

air = load("substrate_integrated_waveguide.step")
substrate = load("substrate_integrated_waveguide_substrate.step").named("substrate")
copper = load("substrate_integrated_waveguide_copper.step").named("copper")
faces = air.boundaries
mesh = TetrahedralMesher(target_size=1.3e-3, embedded_regions=(substrate, copper), refinements=(
    LocalRefinement(region=copper, target_size=0.7e-3), LocalRefinement(region=substrate, target_size=0.8e-3),
    LocalRefinement(region=faces.vias, target_size=0.22e-3))).generate(air)
sheet = region_interface(mesh, copper, substrate)
print(f"Mesh: {mesh.cell_count:,} tetrahedra, {len(mesh.coordinates):,} vertices")

view = plots.Camera(elevation=40, azimuth=-60)
fig = plots.figure(1, 2, size=(14, 6))
plots.geometry([copper, load("substrate_integrated_waveguide_vias.step")], labels=False, camera=view, length_unit="mm",
               title="Top copper and vias (STEP)", ax=fig[0])
plots.mesh(mesh, groups=("all",), legend=False, camera=view, length_unit="mm", title=f"{mesh.cell_count} tetrahedra", ax=fig[1])
plots.show()

Model

Port 1 supplies 1 W. One frequency is below the predicted cutoff and three are above it. The final solve also supplies the field for the phase measurement at 12 GHz.

material = RegionwiseElectromagneticMaterial({"substrate": IsotropicElectromagneticMaterial(relative_permittivity=permittivity)},
                                             default=IsotropicElectromagneticMaterial())
frequencies = np.array([8.0e9, 9.3e9, 10.5e9, 12.0e9])
numerics = Numerics(spatial=FiniteElement(element=Nedelec(order=1)), linear_solver=ComplexSparseDirect())
sweep = {}
for frequency in frequencies:
    m = FrequencyDomainMaxwell(domain=air.volume, frequency=frequency, material=material,
                               phasor_convention="exp(-i*omega*t)", amplitude_convention="peak")
    boundaries = (TEMPort(physics=m, boundary=faces.port_1, signal=sheet, incident_power=1.0),
                  TEMPort(physics=m, boundary=faces.port_2, signal=sheet),
                  PerfectElectricConductor(physics=m, boundary=faces.ground | faces.vias | sheet),
                  ImpedanceBoundary(physics=m, boundary=faces.open, admittance=1 / eta0))
    problem = Problem(name="Via-fenced waveguide", geometry=air, physics=(m,), boundary_conditions=boundaries)
    result = Simulation(problem=problem, mesh=mesh, numerics=numerics).solve_stationary()
    sweep[frequency] = scattering_parameters(result, m)

Results

Transmission and reflection over the band against the cutoff of the equivalent waveguide; the power that is neither reflected nor transmitted leaves through the open top of the box, radiated by the feed lines and tapers. At 12 GHz the reflected wave is weak, so the field in the middle of the guide is one travelling wave: the slope of its phase along the guide is the propagation constant, compared with the equivalent waveguide and with a guide as wide as the via rows.

dB = lambda x: 20 * np.log10(max(abs(x), 1e-6))
effective = rows - diameter**2 / (0.95 * pitch)
cutoff = light / (2 * effective * np.sqrt(permittivity))
print(f"equivalent width {effective * 1e3:.3f} mm, TE10 cutoff {cutoff / 1e9:.3f} GHz")
print("f [GHz]   |S21| [dB]   |S11| [dB]   |S11|^2 + |S21|^2")
for f, S in sweep.items():
    print(f"{f / 1e9:6.2f} {dB(S[(1, 0)]):12.2f} {dB(S[(0, 0)]):12.2f} {abs(S[(0, 0)]) ** 2 + abs(S[(1, 0)]) ** 2:18.4f}")
print(f"below the cutoff ({frequencies[0] / 1e9:.1f} GHz) |S21| = {dB(sweep[frequencies[0]][(1, 0)]):.1f} dB; "
      f"above it ({frequencies[-2] / 1e9:.1f} and {frequencies[-1] / 1e9:.1f} GHz) {dB(sweep[frequencies[-2]][(1, 0)]):.2f} and {dB(sweep[frequencies[-1]][(1, 0)]):.2f} dB")
x = np.linspace(-8e-3, 8e-3, 81)
along = result.field(m.electric_field).evaluate(np.column_stack((x, 0 * x, 0 * x + height / 2)))[:, 2]
phase = np.unwrap(np.angle(along))
k0 = 2 * np.pi * 12e9 / light
beta = {"equivalent width": np.sqrt(permittivity * k0**2 - (np.pi / effective) ** 2), "width of the via rows": np.sqrt(permittivity * k0**2 - (np.pi / rows) ** 2)}
measured = abs(np.polyfit(x, phase, 1)[0])
print(f"propagation constant at 12 GHz from the phase along the guide: {measured:.1f} rad/m; equivalent waveguide {beta['equivalent width']:.1f} rad/m "
      f"({measured / beta['equivalent width'] - 1:+.1%}), guide as wide as the via rows {beta['width of the via rows']:.1f} rad/m ({measured / beta['width of the via rows'] - 1:+.1%})")

Going further

Change the via pitch or the row spacing in substrate_integrated_waveguide_geometry.py: the cutoff follows the equivalent width. With a pitch above twice the diameter the via walls start to leak.
