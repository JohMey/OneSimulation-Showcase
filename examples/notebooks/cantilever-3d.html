Solid mechanics · 3D

A 3D cantilever beam

A clamped 120×18×12 mm block under a 30 N end load: full 3D displacement and von Mises stress, with beam theory as a reference.

3D FEMQ2 hexahedraLinear elasticity
Download the notebook (.ipynb)Download the script (.py)

What it computes

OneSimulation builds the hexahedral mesh, assembles the linear-elastic equilibrium −∇·σ = 0 with an isotropic Hooke law and small strains, and solves for displacement and stress. The clamped face, the integrated total load, symmetry, finite field values, a two-mesh comparison and agreement with the Euler–Bernoulli beam approximation are all checked. Beam theory is an independent plausibility reference, not an exact 3D solution.

What it checks

Euler–Bernoulli beam approximation and a two-mesh comparison.

What you need to run it. An installed, activated OneSimulation runtime (Python 3.13). As a student or course instructor, you have free access. Everyone else can start with the 7-day free trial. The notebook itself carries no key. These are numerical verifications against references, not experimental validation.
