6 Worked physics and service recipes

Each recipe constructs its own model and supplies its own conditions, method and verification. Run the shared cells at the start of this chapter before the recipes that use them. The three helpers serve different contracts. Global histories contain one sample per scalar field and output time. Line profiles retain the location of native coefficients. Service configurations return their own result objects and carriers. The examples in this chapter and the preceding chapters together cover all 73 public physics model declarations in the inspected checkout.
