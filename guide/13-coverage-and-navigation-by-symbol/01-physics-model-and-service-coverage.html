13.1 Physics model and service coverage

This index covers the 73 public model declarations that enter the field-based simulation route. The service links identify independently callable facades and directly exercised providers. Constitutive laws and boundary declarations appear with the cases that consume them and retain their own signatures in Chapter A.

13.1.1 Acoustics

PressureDrivenMode (6.4.1), RadiatingMode (6.4.2), UltrasonicAcoustics (6.4.4), LinearAcoustics (6.4.3).

Service routes include AcousticsPublicApi (6.4.5), StructuralAcousticsPublicApi (6.4.6), UltrasoundPublicApi (6.4.7), VibroacousticsPublicApi (6.4.8).

13.1.2 Biophysics

OrganClearance (6.5.1), AxialTissue (6.5.4), PassiveMembrane (6.5.2), CompliantVessels (6.5.3).

Service routes include BioelectricalPublicApi (6.5.5), CirculatoryPublicApi (6.5.6), OrganPublicApi (6.5.7), TissuePublicApi (6.5.8), BioheatProvider (6.5.9).

13.1.3 Chemistry

SpeciesTransport (5.4), LumpedElectrode (6.6.1), LumpedBattery (6.6.2), ReactionKinetics (6.6.3).

13.1.4 Electromagnetics

FrequencyDomainMaxwell (6.7.5), SeriesRLC (6.7.1), AveragedBuckConverter (6.7.2), DCMotor (6.7.3), MagneticDiffusion (6.7.4).

13.1.7 Heat transfer

HeatConduction (3.2), RadiationCooling (2.1), PhaseChangeHeating (6.10.1), HeatAdvectionDiffusion (6.10.2), ConvectiveHeatTransfer (3.2).
