5.7 Geotechnics

onesim.physics.geotechnics

GroundwaterFlow advances hydraulic head with conductivity, specific storage and recharge. A spatially periodic mode with constant coefficients and zero recharge has mean head conserved and amplitude decaying with diffusivity \(K/S_s\). The shifted line in test_public_groundwater.py checks that the actual coordinate enters the source and initial field. Use FD central difference and central Laplacian on the uniform periodic example. Plot head and Darcy flux against physical distance.

TerzaghiConsolidation describes one-dimensional consolidation with prescribed total stress. BiotConsolidation adds elastic displacement and fluid-content coupling. Distinguish drained pressure conditions, prescribed outward discharge and impermeability. Read pore pressure and displacement together and check a storage or consolidation balance. test_consolidation_method_flexibility.py and test_consolidation_physical_ports.py show concrete declarations. For a saturation front use BuckleyLeverettFlow with a fractional-flow law such as QuadraticCoreyFractionalFlow, a saturation inlet and an outflow. Compare front position and water balance across meshes and inspect the bounds \(0\leq S\leq1\).
