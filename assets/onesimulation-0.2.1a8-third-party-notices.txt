OneSimulation wheel. Their own distributions contain the applicable licenses
and notices. The release verification writes a complete installed distribution
inventory including versions and declared license metadata.

Direct dependencies include NumPy (BSD-3-Clause, with additional bundled-code
notices in its distribution), Numba (BSD-2-Clause), SciPy (BSD-3-Clause, with
additional native dependency notices), h5py (BSD-3-Clause), RFC 8785

An installation or desktop bundle containing native libraries must retain their

## Scientific catalogs

`onesim/physics/plasma/data/chemistry`, `data/transport`, `data/materials` and
generated Python catalogs contain literature citations, data-source records and
per-record provenance. Keep those records with redistributed catalogs. A
citation or a `license` value in project metadata alone is not evidence that a
publisher licensed every cited table for redistribution.

The release tool exports the exact catalog paths, hashes and all provenance
records to `scientific-data-inventory.json`. Publication requires a reviewed
rights record for every catalog. Unreviewed records block the upload gate;
they are not silently treated as covered by the project license. The same rule applies to data
embedded in generated Python modules.

This notice makes no blanket claim of third-party redistribution clearance.

## Scientific facts and mathematical implementations

`LicenseRef-Scientific-Facts` identifies scientific constants, coefficients,
reaction stoichiometry and mathematical relations encoded in the project's own
schema or executable implementation. It is a provenance/review category, not a
claim that the cited publisher granted open or Creative Commons rights to an
article or database. Publication text, illustrations, table layouts, creative
compilations and source-specific database rights remain separate.

Project-authored schema, annotations and code are proprietary (see LICENSE). Keep the original
scientific citations, units, source locations and stated model limitations.
A restricted license on an article does not itself relicense scientific facts;
this category does not permit copying protected parts of that article. The
hash-bound release review must establish the actual scope separately for each
file. Changing a record's license marker alone does not clear an upload.

Raw LXCat/IST-Lisbon catalog extractions remain excluded. No LXCat database,
article PDF, publisher table image or bulk source-database download is bundled.

WarpX helium PIC-MCC cross sections
----------------------------------
The six helium scattering, excitation and ionization tables are unmodified
members of the compressed runtime resource helium-turner.v1.zip, taken from
BLAST-WarpX/warpx-data revision b59a35f8d2d10592b2ea0ea18608dc2a12bc1380
(MCC_cross_sections/He). Their source and individual SHA-256 digests are
recorded in the loader. The upstream copyright, conditions and disclaimer
are reproduced verbatim in onesim/physics/plasma/data/collisions/warpx/LICENSE.txt.
